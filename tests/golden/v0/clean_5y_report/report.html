4 · Example model — London KZ sweep reversal (NY AM)

Rules: between 8.5h and 11h NY, price trades beyond the London killzone high (low); within 6 bars an M5 candle closes back inside → enter at that close, opposite direction. Stop 1 pip beyond the sweep extreme. Target 2R. Flat by 12:00. One trade per day. Costs ≥ 1 pip round trip (or the recorded spread + 0.2). If stop and target are touched in the same bar, the stop is assumed (conservative).
